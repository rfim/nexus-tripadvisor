Different market, same job.

Why Lifepal's insurance marketplace maps onto Tripadvisor's travel marketplace. In Lifepal, the other side was the insurers; at Tripadvisor, it's hotels, restaurants, experiences and the booking partners behind every "check prices" click.

Both businesses sit in the middle. Lifepal helped people compare insurance and sent them on to the insurers that cover them; Tripadvisor helps travellers compare places and sends them on to the partners that sell the stay. In both, events arrive late or twice, partners describe the same thing in their own shapes, records change after the fact, people can ask to be forgotten, and partner revenue depends on every click being counted once. The data problems that follow are the ones NEXUS was built to solve.
