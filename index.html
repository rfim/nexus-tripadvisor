One review, version by version

    
A reviewer edits, then asks to be erased. A replay re-sends the old edit with a new id, so dedupe misses it. The version rule doesn't: the tombstone is version 4, and nothing older brings the review back.

07 · Stream, then batchthe batch checks the stream; it doesn't replace it

    
Fast first. Right by morning.

      
The stream publishes in minutes; the nightly batch recomputes every window from the whole topic. Every difference must be explained, record for record, by the side output.

    

    

      
Windows that differ, and why
An iOS app flushed a flight's worth of events hours late. The stream had closed those windows; the batch counts them.

      
Page views per hour, and the hour held
An hour over 2σ and double its usual volume is held from Gold. One was: three users, 1,500 views.

    

  






  

    
08 · SLAsmeasured the way a consumer feels them

    
On time is a number. So is late.

      
Freshness is how far published completeness lags, so a quiet 3 a.m. still reads as current. Sampled every five minutes; the SLO sets the error budget.

    

    
partner_revenue_15m on Friday 9 October
Minutes behind vs the 45-minute SLA. A bad record stopped the job at 14:05; freshness crossed the line at 14:35. A restart alert would have paged at 14:08.
