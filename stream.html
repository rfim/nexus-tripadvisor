Kafka to the warehouse. One record at a time.

A record lands on a partition. The job reads it, de-duplicates it, puts it in a window by event time, and fires the window when the watermark passes. What doesn't fit goes somewhere on purpose. Pick a problem and step through it.

Pick a problem above, then press Play or step through.
    The watermark

    min over active partitions − out-of-orderness

    A partition with nothing for five minutes stops holding it back. When every partition is quiet, processing time carries it, so a 3 a.m. window still closes.

    Lateness

    Re-fire within the hour, side output after

    A window keeps its state an hour past the watermark. A record inside that hour updates the window and fires it again; the sink upserts. After it, the batch is the place.

    Exactly once, from outside

    Checkpointed state, keyed sinks

    Offsets, windows, dedupe state and the ledger are restored together. The sink and side outputs are keyed, so what a restore re-sends changes nothing.
